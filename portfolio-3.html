About

I'm a brand marketer with an MSc (Distinction) from King's College London and a First Class BA in Spanish & Chinese Studies from the University of Nottingham. I speak five languages and have lived and studied across Europe, Taiwan and Spain.

Most recently I was the sole marketing hire for JoyBuy's UEFA Champions League sponsorship activation — managing the full campaign from Matchday 1 through to the Budapest Final across UK, France, Germany and the Netherlands.

My background in cross-cultural research and language gives me a different lens on brand strategy — particularly in Asian and multilingual European markets.
