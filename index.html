FAQ

Questions before you book the call.

How quickly will I see results?+

Paid campaigns typically start generating qualified leads within the first 2-3 weeks; appointment setting adds booked calls on top of that as soon as outbound is live. We agree clear milestones during the audit so you know what "working" looks like before we start.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. We don't lock clients into long contracts — we'd rather earn the right to keep working with you every month based on results.

What size of business do you work with?+

We work primarily with SMEs and B2B service businesses that have a clear offer and sales process, and want to scale a predictable acquisition channel rather than rely on referrals alone.

Do you actually make the sales calls, or just generate leads?+

Both, depending on the plan. Our team can run outbound calling and appointment setting so qualified, confirmed calls land directly on your team's calendar — you focus on closing.

How is reporting handled?+

You get regular reporting tied to booked calls, cost per booking and pipeline influenced — not just clicks and impressions — plus a direct line to your account manager.

How many new clients do you take on?+

We onboard a limited number of new clients each month so every account gets a dedicated team and proper ramp-up — book a call to check current availability.
